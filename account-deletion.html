How to request deletion

SpendlyAI does not yet offer an in-app "Delete Account" button. To delete your account and associated data, send a deletion request using the form below, or email us directly at haideralidev258@gmail.com from the email address associated with your SpendlyAI account.

What gets deleted

When you request account deletion, we permanently delete:

  • Your account (email and login credentials)
  • Your profile information (name)
  • Your expense records (amounts, notes, dates, categories)
  • Your custom spending categories

We do not retain any of this data after deletion is processed, and it is not shared with any third party.

Timeframe

Deletion requests are processed manually and completed within 30 days of receipt. You'll receive a confirmation email once your account and data have been deleted.

Request form

This opens your email app with a pre-filled message addressed to haideralidev258@gmail.com. Review it and hit send.
